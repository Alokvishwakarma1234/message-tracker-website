Good to know

Message Tracker is not a bank.

यह बैंक नहीं है। यह आपके अपने बैंक के SMS से आपका हिसाब रखने वाला ऐप है।

It does not open an account, hold your money or link to your bank account. When the app says “Add bank”, it only means: tell me which of your own banks to follow, so I can read that bank’s SMS on your phone and work out how much money you have and where it went.

  • Not a bank, not a wallet, not a UPI app
  • No bank login, nothing gets linked
  • A private tracker for the bank SMS already on your phone

Part 1

Getting started

शुरुआत: 5 आसान steps
  1. Step 1

    Open the app: welcome and privacy

    पहली स्क्रीन: ऐप क्या करता है, साफ़ शब्दों में

    The first screen sums up the privacy promise in plain words: no internet permission, data stored encrypted on the phone, no ads, OTPs hidden behind your phone lock. Below it are the permissions, each with its reason.

    What to tap

    • Allow (SMS, needed)Reads your inbox and new SMS to find payments, salary, OTPs and alerts. It never edits, deletes or sends SMS.
    • Allow (Contacts, optional)Shows names and photos instead of bare numbers.
    • Allow (Notifications, optional)A short note when a bank or UPI SMS is recorded.
    • Purana data wapas laaoRestore your data from a backup file, for example on a new phone.
    • Privacy PolicyOpens the policy in your browser.
    • Scan my SMSStarts reading once SMS is allowed. Until then the button says “Allow SMS to continue”.

    Location for payments stays off. You can turn it on later in Settings.

  2. Step 2

    SMS permission, explained first

    Android के popup से पहले एक ज़रूरी बात

    Before Android’s own permission popup, the app shows exactly what it will read (SMS already on the phone and new ones: sender, text, time) and why: to separate spends, income, balance, salary, bills and OTPs and build your reports. The same text is shown in English too.

    What to tap

    • Samajh gaya, aage badheinOpens Android’s popup. Tap Allow there.
    • Abhi nahiGoes back without giving the permission.

    The same kind of screen appears before Contacts and SIM info.

  3. Step 3

    Mera number: choose your SIM

    Dual SIM? सिर्फ़ आपके SIM के messages

    If the phone has two SIMs, pick yours so only your SIM’s SMS are counted. Add your name and your 10-digit mobile number (without +91).

    What to tap

    • Allow SIM infoReads only which SIMs are in the phone (slot, carrier, number if Android gives it). No calls or SMS are made.
    • Choose your SIMSIM 1 or SIM 2. Only that SIM’s SMS are counted.
    • Purane / unknown SIM ke messages bhi dikhaoAlso show SMS with no SIM tag, or from a SIM no longer in the phone.
    • Save & continueSaves and moves on.
    • Skip, show all SIMsMoves on without filtering by SIM.

    You can change this later in Settings, Mera number.

  4. Step 4

    Set your app PIN

    अपना 4-digit PIN, फ़ोन का pattern नहीं

    Choose a 4-digit PIN just for Message Tracker (not your phone’s lock). It is asked when the app opens and after 30 seconds away. Enter it twice to confirm.

    What to tap

    • Skip for nowOpens the app without a PIN. Set one later in Settings, App Lock.
    • PIN bhool gaye?On the lock screen: confirm it is you with your phone’s screen lock or fingerprint, then set a new PIN. Your data stays.
    • Unlock with fingerprintAvailable once you turn on “Fingerprint se bhi kholo” in Settings. The PIN always works.

    Too many wrong tries make the app wait a little before the next try.

  5. Step 5

    Add your bank: tell the app which bank is yours

    Bank add = ऐप को बताना कि आपका बैंक कौन-सा है

    This does not open an account and nothing gets linked. Money is counted only from accounts you add here, so add the bank you really use and the balance you really have. Open it from Home, “Bank account add karein” (also in Money, and in Settings, Mere bank accounts).

    What to tap

    • Bank name (type or choose)Type your bank’s name or tap it in the list.
    • A/c last 4 digitsAs shown in your bank SMS (XX1234). It matches the right SMS to this account.
    • Current balance (₹)What is in the account now.
    • Balance as ofThe date and time of that balance. Only SMS after this time are added.
    • Save balanceDone. From now on the balance updates from that bank’s SMS on its own.
    • Cash in handA separate wallet for cash. Cash is not in SMS, so add money in or out with Adjust.

    Accounts the app spots in your SMS show under My Balance as “found in your SMS”. Tap one to fill the form.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Message Tracker a bank? Does it connect to my bank account?

No to both. Message Tracker is not a bank and never links to your bank account. “Add bank” only tells the app which of your own banks to follow: you type the bank’s name, the last 4 digits from its SMS and your current balance. The app then reads that bank’s SMS on your phone, offline. Use it with the bank account you really use, and it shows your real balance and spending. यह बैंक नहीं है। “Add bank” का मतलब सिर्फ़ इतना है कि ऐप को बताइए आपका कौन-सा बैंक है, ताकि वह उसके SMS से आपका हिसाब रख सके।

Does Message Tracker need the internet?

No. The app does not have the internet permission at all, so it cannot send your SMS or money data anywhere. Everything is worked out on your phone.

Which messages does it read?

Only SMS from the bank accounts you add. Before asking for SMS access, the app explains in plain Hindi and English what it reads and why.

Are there ads or a sign-up?

No ads, no advertising ID, no account and no sign-up. Open it, tell it which bank you use, and you are done.

How do I back up my data?

Backup goes to a file you pick yourself through Android’s own file picker, for example your own Google Drive or a folder on your phone. Restore from the same file on your new phone.

What is the Savdhaan chip?

Links inside your SMS are clickable. When a link looks risky, the app puts a “Savdhaan” chip next to it, so you think twice before tapping.

Can it move money or see my bank password?

No. It cannot move money, and it never asks for your bank password, UPI PIN, OTP, net-banking login or full account number. It only reads the SMS already on your phone.

Is it available on Google Play?

It is in closed beta on Google Play right now. Tap “Join the beta”, choose “Become a tester”, and then install it from the Play Store.
